# EcoShift VR

EcoShift VR is an embodied interactive virtual-reality system developed for environmental education through non-human perspectives.

The system contains two ecological scenarios:

- **Terrestrial:** users experience the perspective of a Sunda pangolin, encountering environmental pollution and poaching threats.
- **Marine:** users experience the perspective of a blue shark, encountering marine pollution and fishing-net/bycatch threats.

EcoShift VR was developed as part of the research presented in:

> **Beyond Knowledge Transfer: Non-Human Embodiment for Environmental Education**  
> Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction (PACMHCI), ISS 2026.

## Research Overview

The project investigates how an embodied interactive VR experience compares with lecture-style informational video for environmental education.

The study uses a 2 × 2 design crossing:

- Medium: Embodied Interactive VR vs. Lecture-Style Informational Video
- Ecosystem: Terrestrial vs. Marine

The VR system combines first-person perspective, species-specific locomotion,environmental interaction, action-contingent feedback, and conservation education.
